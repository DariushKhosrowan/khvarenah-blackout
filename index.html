Why Khvarenah?

Most VPNs and circumvention tools fail during a national blackout because they assume some direct foreign route still exists. Khvarenah is engineered for the worst case: it dynamically discovers paths, races them in parallel, and degrades gracefully through four tiers of resilience — even when every standard TCP/UDP route to international IPs is severed.

The Resilience Ladder

Level 1 — Stealth foreign egress

VLESS-XTLS / REALITY and Shadowsocks-2022 (AEAD) with browser-fingerprint TLS. Timeout: 1.5 s.

Level 2 — Pluggable transports & domestic relays

Tor via WebTunnel / Snowflake, and Shir-o-Khorshid over an in-country VPS. Timeout: 3.0 s.

Level 3 — Native multipath DNS tunneling

The blackout workhorse: queries striped across a dynamic pool of domestic ISP recursive resolvers, QNAME ≤101 bytes, base32, low-overhead ARQ.

Level 4 — Intranet P2P mesh

mDNS discovery of neighboring Khvarenah nodes with a working uplink, and local hop forwarding.

Key engineered defenses

  • TUN anti-loop routing — SO_MARK/fwmark on Linux, WinTUN bypass on Windows, VpnService.protect on Android.
  • Authenticated DNS-tunnel responses — ChaCha20-Poly1305 / Noise; poisoned resolvers are rejected and penalized.
  • Liveness sentinel — 4s of zero inbound bytes triggers soft-break and failover against silent black-holing.
  • MTU / MSS clamping — MSS ≤1200, DNS responses ≤1232 bytes to defeat fragmentation drops.
  • Interface-flap handling — reacts to netlink changes, flushes and re-probes the resolver pool.
  • Entropy shaping — paced, padded, decoy-mixed queries so tunnel traffic matches ordinary DNS stats.
